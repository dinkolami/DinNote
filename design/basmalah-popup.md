# DinNote — Basmalah detail popup (approved)

Design approved 2026-11-16. Apply to the Basmalah detail popup and reuse as the style for similar reading dialogs.

- White/off-white background; compact rounded corners (approximately 12–16 dp), not oversized pill corners.
- Thin clean turquoise/teal outer border (approximately 1 dp), subtle and minimalist.
- Reduce excessive blank space and vertical height; use content-wrapping layout with safe scrolling for long content.
- Title 'Basmalah', Arabic Bismillah right-aligned RTL, transliteration, Malay meaning, reference and usage note, in that order.
- Place a delicate Islamic architectural arch/mosque silhouette decorative footer *after the final text*, with restrained turquoise and pale gold accents; ornament must not overlap text or controls.
- Small outlined turquoise 'Tutup' button at bottom right.
- Preserve correct Arabic diacritics and readable text.
